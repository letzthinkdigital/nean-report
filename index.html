52 of 54 adds to cart did not turn into a purchase. The carousel is doing its job: luxury shoppers find the FW26 pieces and add them to cart. The sale is lost in two places: 49 carts never reached checkout, and 3 of the 5 shoppers who started checkout did not finish. Saudi Arabia holds 31 of the 54 carts and both purchases.
Why Shoppers Are Not Buying | Likely Reasons
💰
Price is the most likely barrier  Major factor
The two orders averaged $319.45, a high ticket for a first online purchase. The ad set targets luxury shoppers (Chanel, Dior, Hermès interests), who add to cart readily but compare before paying. The drop happens at the cart, where the full total becomes real.
🚚
Kuwait, Qatar and UAE carts are not converting
Outside Saudi Arabia, 23 adds to cart led to 3 checkouts and no purchase. Delivery fees, delivery times or payment options in those markets are worth comparing with what Saudi shoppers see.
🧾
Checkout friction
3 of 5 shoppers who started checkout left. Account creation, address forms or a payment error at this final step are worth testing on an iPhone in each market.
💳
Payment options
Without buy now, pay later (such as Tabby or Tamara) or Apple Pay, a basket near $300 is harder to commit to on the spot.
📏
Size, fit and returns doubts
For a new season collection bought online, uncertainty about size and how easy returns are across borders makes people save items rather than buy.
🛍️
Browsing and comparing
Many shoppers use the cart as a wishlist while they compare prices or wait for a sale, especially with White Friday and year-end sales coming up.
Recommended Actions
  1. Retarget everyone who added to cart. Run a catalog (dynamic) ad to people who added to cart in the last 14 days but did not buy, showing the exact FW26 pieces they left.
  2. Move the video budget to the carousel. The carousel produced 52 of 54 adds to cart at $15.39 each and both purchases; the video produced 2 adds to cart at $97.87 each. Replace the video with a second carousel variation.
  3. Test more budget in Riyadh. Saudi Arabia has both purchases (2.08x ROAS) and the cheapest add to cart ($9.90). Two purchases is still a small sample, so raise it in steps and judge on carts and checkouts.
  4. Review Kuwait, Capital Governorate first. Kuwait took the most spend ($337.82) at the highest cost per add to cart ($42.23). Capital alone took $130.93 at $1.77 per click, against $0.86 in Ahmadi and $0.89 in Farwaniya.
  5. Show the full price upfront. Make delivery fees, duties and any free-delivery threshold for KSA, Qatar and UAE clear on the product page and in the ad copy.
  6. Test checkout with the e-commerce team on an iPhone in each market: payment methods (Apple Pay, Tabby or Tamara), address fields for each country, stock and sizes for the most added products.
  7. Fix the setup items listed under How to Read This Report (iOS filter and optimisation event) so the campaign does what its name says.

Key Observations
🇸🇦
Saudi Arabia is the standout market
Riyadh produced both purchases ($638.89, 2.08x ROAS) and 31 of 54 adds to cart at $9.90 each, with the lowest CPM of the four markets ($11.04).
🇰🇼
Kuwait is the most expensive per cart
Kuwait took $337.82 (33.9%), the largest share, for 8 adds to cart at $42.23 each and no purchase, even though its CTR (all) is the best at 1.56%. Clicks are not turning into carts.
🏆
The carousel is doing almost all the work
The carousel has 52 of 54 adds to cart at $15.39 each and both purchases. The video has a higher CTR (1.55%) but only 2 adds to cart for $195.74.
📈
Delivery got much cheaper from 14 Nov
Daily reach jumped from about 1,700 to 17,417 on 15 Nov at the same spend. CPM fell from $30.96 to $9.11 across the two periods, with no changes logged. Carts grew and both purchases followed, but link CTR halved.
🛒
The leak is at cart and checkout
54 adds to cart led to 5 checkouts and 2 purchases. Unlike a cart-only drop, 3 of 5 checkouts were also abandoned, so the payment and delivery step needs checking too.
👥
35 to 44 is the core cart audience
The 35 to 44 group added 33 of 54 carts at $12.98 each and one purchase; the other came from 30 to 34. 45 to 54 is the weakest: $261.07 for 5 adds to cart ($52.21 each).
How to Read This Report
  1. The campaign went live on 5 November 2026. The range runs from 30 October, but there was no delivery from 30 Oct to 4 Nov. 17 Nov is a partial day (data pulled at about 14:15 Kuwait time, marked *). Figures use the default attribution setting (7-day click, 1-day view, 1-day engaged view), so late purchases can still be added to recent days.
  2. Results are website purchases, not adds to cart. The campaign name says "Conversions (ATC)", but Meta reports its result as website purchases, which is why Results shows 2. Adds to cart (54) are reported separately. If the intent was to optimise for Add to Cart, the ad set conversion event needs changing.
  3. No iOS filter found in the targeting. The names say "iOS Devices", but the ad set targeting returned by Meta shows mobile devices only, with no operating system filter. Android users may be included. Worth checking in Ads Manager.
  4. Placements are open, with Instagram weighted. The ad set is named "Placement = Insta", but all placements are on. A value rule raises Instagram feed bids by 150% and lowers Facebook feed, Stories, Reels and Audience Network by 90%, so 99.3% of spend went to Instagram in practice.
  5. App installs are 0. No app install events were attributed to this campaign. The figure is shown as recorded, not estimated.
  6. Meta does not return conversions with an area breakdown. Adds to cart, checkouts and purchases come back as not available by area, so area efficiency is ranked on cost per link click. Saudi Arabia delivered only in Riyadh Region, as targeted.
  7. Reach does not add up across breakdowns. Country reach sums to 36,934 against a campaign reach of 37,707, and area reach sums differ from country reach. Carousel and Video reach in each area overlap, because one person can see both ads.
  8. Age 30-34 and 55 are shown inside Meta's 25-34 and 55-64 brackets (marked *). The ad set targets women aged 30 to 55, so only those ages are included.
  9. Sharjah is not targeted directly. It receives delivery because the Dubai target uses a 17 km radius that reaches into Sharjah. 3 content views have no country attributed and are included only in the campaign total.
